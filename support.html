Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No! Glug works entirely on your device. We don't require any sign-ups, and all your hydration data is stored securely and privately on your phone.

How do I sync with Apple Watch?

The Glug Apple Watch app syncs automatically with your iPhone as long as they are paired and nearby. Just open the app on your watch to log your water!

Is my data shared with anyone?

Never. Glug respects your privacy. If you enable HealthKit, we only write your water intake to the Apple Health app on your device. We do not collect or sell your data.
