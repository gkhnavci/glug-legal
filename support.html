Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No — Glug works entirely on your device. There's no sign-up, and all your hydration data stays stored privately on your phone.

How do I sync with Apple Watch?

The Glug Apple Watch app syncs automatically with your iPhone as long as they're paired and nearby. Just open the app on your watch to log water.

Is my data shared with anyone?

Never. Glug has no servers and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. If you enable HealthKit, water intake is written only to the Apple Health app on your own device.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple. Go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions to view, change, or cancel.
