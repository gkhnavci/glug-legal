Three steps, every day

No setup, no streak-breaking friction — just the habit, made a little more alive.

Why Glug

Small details, real habit change

Everything here is built to make the boring part of drinking enough water feel a little bit alive.

Color-reactive mascot

Water tints blue, coffee runs deep brown, tea turns leaf green. Your buddy reflects your actual day — not a generic progress bar.

A growing squad

Dozens of mascots to unlock, each with its own personality across happy, neutral, and thirsty states.

Streaks that mean it

Real consistency tracking with badges that reward showing up again, not just one good afternoon.

On your wrist

The Apple Watch companion logs a glass in one tap and syncs instantly — no need to reach for your phone.

In step with Health

Optional HealthKit sync keeps your water intake aligned with the rest of your Health data.

Nothing leaves your device

No accounts, no ad SDKs, no third-party analytics, no server — Glug doesn't have one to send your data to.
