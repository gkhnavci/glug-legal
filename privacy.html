Glug

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Glug ("the app") is a hydration tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This page explains what information the app handles and how. In short: your hydration and profile data stays on your device — Glug has no servers of its own and does not require an account. The one exception is advertising: the free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, described in the Advertising section below. Glug Pro subscribers see no ads and are not affected by that section at all.

Information the app handles

  • Hydration entries you log (drink type, amount, time) — stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage. This data is never sent to us because we have nowhere to send it to.
  • Profile details you enter (name, weight, daily goal, language, sound/notification preferences) — stored locally on your device to personalize the app and calculate your daily water goal.
  • Apple Health data — if you grant permission, Glug can read and write water intake (dietaryWater) to the Health app. This only happens with your explicit permission, and access is managed entirely through iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices, where you can revoke it at any time.
  • Apple Watch sync — if you use the Glug Watch app, your hydration totals sync directly between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This is a direct device-to-device connection; it does not pass through any third-party server.
  • Purchases — subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Glug never sees or stores your payment information; we only receive confirmation of what you've unlocked.
  • Advertising data (free version only) — if you are not a Glug Pro subscriber, the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. This involves your device's advertising identifier (only if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission), general device/ad-interaction data, and — for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland — a consent choice collected through Google's User Messaging Platform. See the Advertising section below for details.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device based on your own activity. They do not rely on a push notification server and no notification content is sent anywhere outside your device.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Your hydration and profile data is never shared with anyone because it never leaves your device. The parties involved in running Glug are Apple's own frameworks (HealthKit, StoreKit, WatchConnectivity, local notifications), governed by Apple's privacy terms for those services, and — for free-tier users only — Google AdMob and its advertising partners, as described in the Advertising section above.

Your choices

  • You can revoke Apple Health access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Glug.
  • You can turn off notifications and sounds from within the app's Settings screen.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored hydration and profile data from your device.
  • You can change your App Tracking Transparency choice for Glug at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and manage the AdMob consent choice by clearing the app's data or reinstalling it.
  • Subscribing to Glug Pro removes ads entirely, along with all the advertising data collection described above.

Children's privacy

Glug is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
